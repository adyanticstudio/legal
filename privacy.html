Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026 | Last Updated: 30 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Radioage. This Privacy Policy explains how Naveen Kumar Boopalan, sole proprietor trading as Adyantic Studio ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("Radioage" or "the App"). Adyantic Studio is the business name under which Radioage is developed and operated.

Radioage does not require you to create an account. We do not intentionally ask you to provide direct contact details such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the App. However, certain information is processed by the App and its service providers to provide radio, location-related, subscription, and diagnostic features.

2. Information We Process and How We Use It

A. Precise Location Data

Radioage uses device location for the "Stations Near You" and "Stations › Near Me" features. The App requests location permission when needed. If permission has already been granted, the Stations screen may read your current or last-known location automatically when that screen opens and when the App returns to the foreground.

  • On-device use: Coordinates are used on your device to calculate distances to radio stations and filter results. They are held in memory for this purpose and are not saved by Radioage as a persistent location history.
  • Geocoding: When you use the location setting that needs geographic information, coordinates may be passed to the device platform's geocoding service to determine information such as a country. This uses Apple's service on iOS and the device's system geocoder on Android, which is commonly provided by Google.
  • Stations › Near Me: This feature uses location on-device to calculate nearby stations. Its location reading is not sent to Radio Browser or radio station servers by Radioage.
  • What we do not do: Radioage does not upload your precise GPS coordinates to Adyantic Studio's own servers, send them to Radio Browser, or maintain a server-side location history.

You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. Location-dependent features will not work without the required permission.

B. Approximate Country from IP Address

When you launch Radioage and when you return to it after it has been in the background, the App uses GeoJS and, if necessary, Cloudflare as a fallback to determine your approximate country from your public IP address.

  • Purpose: The country code may be used to select an initial country, maintain regional availability, and apply geographic restrictions required for radio-station licensing.
  • Information transmitted: The request necessarily exposes your public IP address and standard network information to the geolocation provider. The App uses the country code returned; it does not read or save the IP address as an app profile field.
  • Provider handling: GeoJS and Cloudflare process the request under their own service terms and privacy practices.

C. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Radioage stores app preferences and listening-related information locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Favourite stations and saved station details, including station ID, name, stream and artwork URLs, tags, vote count, country, language, bitrate, station-provided coordinates, frequency, and band.
  • Your selected country, theme and dark palette, language filters, and the "Stations Near You" setting and radius.
  • Regional availability information, such as the detected country and whether a regional restriction applies.
  • The last station and radio band used, and your Recently Played list, which stores up to 15 stations. The list does not store listening timestamps.
  • Listening-mode preferences, including the selected mode (Radio, Genre, or Curated), selected genre or curated list, and the last station associated with a genre or curated list.
  • Collections you create, including collection names, station lists, creation times, the active collection, and the last station in each collection. Collections are stored locally; collection IDs are not used as device identifiers.
  • Certain interface preferences, such as when an upgrade prompt was last shown, and the last-known Radioage PRO status.

Local storage: This information is stored on your device using the App's local storage. Adyantic Studio does not maintain a server-side database of these preferences, collections, favourites, or listening state. Some temporary operational state, including the current GPS coordinates and sleep-timer end time, is held in memory rather than saved as persistent app data.

Search: Station search is performed locally against station information already downloaded by the App. Search terms are not sent to Radioage or to Radio Browser by the search feature.

D. Subscription and Purchase Information

If you purchase a Radioage PRO subscription or other in-app purchase, payment is processed by Apple or Google through the applicable app store. RevenueCat provides subscription-management services used by Radioage to validate purchases, check subscription status, manage renewals and restore purchases, and enable or disable PRO features.

  • Anonymous identifier: Radioage does not require an account or provide RevenueCat with a custom account ID. RevenueCat creates an anonymous App User ID to associate purchase and subscription information with an anonymous customer record.
  • Information handled: RevenueCat and the app stores may process purchase tokens or receipts, product and entitlement information, transaction and expiry dates, store information, subscription status, and technical information needed to operate the service.
  • iOS device identifier: The RevenueCat iOS SDK includes Apple's Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) in its API request headers. The App does not set IDFV as a RevenueCat subscriber attribute. RevenueCat may process request information, including technical identifiers, for service operation, monitoring, and troubleshooting under its own privacy and retention practices.
  • Android identifier backup: On Android, the RevenueCat SDK may back up the anonymous App User ID using Google's Block Store so purchases can be restored after reinstalling or on a new device.
  • Payment credentials: Radioage does not collect or store your card number, bank account number, or other direct payment credentials. Those are handled by the relevant app store and its payment providers.

For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and the privacy information provided by Apple or Google.

E. Crash Reporting and Diagnostic Information (Sentry)

Radioage uses Sentry, a third-party crash reporting and error-monitoring service, to help identify, diagnose, and fix technical errors and improve app stability. In supported store builds, diagnostic events may be sent automatically when the App encounters a reportable error, application crash, Android Application Not Responding (ANR) event, or iOS app-hang event. Ordinary development builds do not send events under the production Sentry configuration.

Information that may be included: Diagnostic events may contain error messages and stack traces; app version, build, release, and environment information; device model and operating-system details; language or locale; time zone; memory, storage, and screen information; and selected technical breadcrumbs describing recent app activity, app lifecycle, connectivity, or system events. The precise contents depend on the event, platform, and SDK.

Information and features not intentionally collected: Sentry sessions, performance tracing, replay, screenshots, view-hierarchy capture, and audio recording are not enabled in the current Radioage configuration. Radioage does not intentionally attach your name, email address, account details, purchase credentials, or RevenueCat App User ID to diagnostic events.

Privacy safeguards: Radioage sets Sentry's sendDefaultPii option to false and removes selected user and device identifiers from JavaScript and supported native events. URL-bearing HTTP breadcrumbs are dropped. In Radioage Android 1.7.0 (versionCode 53) and iOS 1.7.0 (build 6), and later versions that include this safeguard, the App also applies URL redaction to supported event text fields, including error messages, exception causes, breadcrumbs, and extra data, before events are sent. Earlier versions do not include this URL-redaction change. This redaction is a technical safeguard; it should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every possible piece of text generated by every third-party component is free of personal or sensitive information.

IP address and geographic data: Sentry necessarily receives network traffic when an event is transmitted. The current Sentry organisation settings enable prevention of IP-address storage, required data scrubbing, default scrubbing, and Enhanced Privacy. These are Sentry-side configuration controls and may be subject to Sentry's service behaviour and configuration changes.

Processing location and retention: Radioage's Sentry organisation is configured in the United States. Under the Sentry Developer (free) plan currently used by Adyantic Studio, diagnostic events are retained for 30 days. The retention period and available controls may change if the account plan, service settings, or Sentry's terms change. Radioage does not intentionally store Sentry crash-report attachments. See Sentry's Privacy Policy and Sentry's data-retention documentation.

3. Third-Party Services and Network Connections

Radioage relies on third-party services and radio-station infrastructure to provide its features. Those services may receive your IP address and other standard network information when your device connects to them.

  • Radio Browser API (radio-browser.info): Provides the community-maintained radio-station directory and station information. Radioage sends requests for station and country catalogues and uses the country code when requesting a country's station list. Requests use a Radioage/<version> User-Agent. When playback starts, Radioage sends a station "click" request containing the station ID to Radio Browser. Radio Browser may process request information under its own policies.
  • GeoJS (geojs.io) and Cloudflare (cloudflare.com): Used to determine approximate country from your public IP address, with Cloudflare used as a fallback when required.
  • RevenueCat (revenuecat.com): Provides purchase and subscription-management services, as described in Section 2.D.
  • Platform geocoding services: Apple's geocoding service on iOS and the device's system geocoder on Android may receive location coordinates when a feature requests geographic information, as described in Section 2.A.
  • Google connectivity check: On iOS, the network-status library may make connectivity-check requests to clients3.google.com/generate_204. These requests expose standard network information, including your IP address, to Google. Android uses the operating system's network validation rather than this app-level Google probe.
  • Radio stations, broadcasters, and content servers: When you listen, your device connects directly to the relevant station or its service provider to receive the audio stream and, where available, now-playing metadata and station artwork. While playback is active, the App may request now-playing metadata from the station's server periodically (approximately every 15 seconds). Those servers receive standard network information, including your IP address.

Unencrypted connections: Some radio stations provide streams or metadata over HTTP rather than HTTPS. Where a station uses HTTP, that connection is not encrypted in transit. The security and privacy practices of the station and its hosting providers are outside Radioage's control.

These third-party services and station operators have their own privacy policies and terms. Adyantic Studio does not control how independent radio stations, broadcasters, geocoding providers, network services, or content-hosting providers handle information they receive.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Adyantic Studio does not sell or rent your personal information. Radioage does not use the information described in this policy for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the App's features, including:

  • Network information, including IP address, when your device contacts Radio Browser, GeoJS, Cloudflare, Google connectivity services on iOS, RevenueCat, radio-station servers, and other infrastructure.
  • Location coordinates with the platform geocoding service when a location feature requires geographic information. The Stations › Near Me distance calculation is performed on-device.
  • Purchase, subscription, and anonymous customer-identifier information with Apple, Google, and RevenueCat to process and manage purchases.
  • Diagnostic events and technical app/device information with Sentry when a reportable error or crash occurs.

If you choose to share a station using your device's share sheet, Radioage passes the station name and a store link to the platform share interface. You decide whether and with whom to share it.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the App, or Adyantic Studio.

5. Data Retention

Radioage's preferences, favourites, collections, listening-mode choices, Recently Played list, and other app state are stored locally on your device and remain there until you remove the App or clear its local data, subject to the operating system's storage behaviour. On Android, the RevenueCat SDK may separately back up the anonymous App User ID using Google's Block Store so purchases can be restored after reinstalling or on a new device; removing the App may therefore not remove that backed-up identifier.

Radioage does not maintain its own server-side database of these local preferences or listening records. The App does not maintain a timestamped listening-history service.

RevenueCat's anonymous App User ID and cached subscription information are stored by the SDK and processed by RevenueCat under its own privacy policy and retention practices. Purchase records are also subject to the applicable app-store policies.

Sentry diagnostic events are retained according to the account plan and service settings in effect at the time. Sentry controls this retention and may change it if the account plan or service settings change. Diagnostic reports may be temporarily queued on your device while offline and sent when connectivity becomes available.

Your choices

  • Location: You can revoke location permission through your device settings. Location-dependent features will no longer work without permission.
  • Local app data: You can remove Radioage's locally stored app data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data through your device settings, subject to operating-system behaviour. On Android, the RevenueCat SDK may retain a backup of the anonymous App User ID through Google's Block Store for purchase restoration.

  • Performance of a contract or steps taken at your request: to provide the App features you choose to use, manage purchases and subscriptions, and provide related support.
  • Consent: where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, including certain uses of device location. You can withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Legitimate interests: to maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App and its services, where those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is necessary to comply with applicable legal requirements.

The applicable basis depends on the specific processing activity and the law that applies to you. Where we rely on legitimate interests, those interests include operating and securing Radioage, diagnosing technical problems, and protecting the service from misuse.
