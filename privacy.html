Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Radioage. This Privacy Policy explains how Adyantic Studio ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application ("Radioage" or "the App").

Radioage does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Precise Location Data (GPS)

If you choose to enable the "Stations Near You" feature, Radioage will ask for your permission to access your device's precise location (GPS coordinates).

  • How it is used: Your coordinates are processed on your device to calculate the distance between you and available radio stations using mathematical (Haversine) formulas. This allows the app to filter stations within your chosen radius.
  • How it is protected: Your precise location is not uploaded to our servers, and Radioage does not send your GPS coordinates to Radio Browser or other radio services. The location filtering is performed locally on your device.

B. Approximate Location Data (IP Address)

When you launch the app for the first time, Radioage uses the third-party GeoJS service to determine your approximate country based on your IP address.

  • How it is used: The result is used to automatically select a default radio station country so you can start listening without manually selecting a country.
  • What is transmitted: The request to GeoJS necessarily includes the network information required to make the request, including your IP address. GeoJS states that it does not currently store access logs and uses the request to provide IP-based geographic information.

C. Local App Data

Radioage stores certain app preferences locally on your device. This includes:

  • Your saved favorite stations.
  • Your selected theme (Light/Dark).
  • Your chosen language filters and radius settings.
  • Your selected default country.

How it is protected: This information is stored locally on your device using the app's local storage. Radioage does not maintain a server-side database containing these preferences.

D. Subscription and Purchase Information

If you purchase a Radioage PRO subscription, the transaction is processed by the applicable app store (Apple App Store or Google Play Store) and subscription information is managed through RevenueCat.

  • How it is used: RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription information to validate transactions, maintain subscription status, and enable or disable Radioage PRO features.
  • Anonymous identifier: Because Radioage does not require an account or provide RevenueCat with a custom user identifier, RevenueCat generates an anonymous App User ID for the installation. This identifier is used by RevenueCat to associate purchase and subscription information with that anonymous customer record.
  • Payment information: Radioage does not collect or store your credit card number, bank account number, or other direct payment credentials. Payment processing is handled by Apple or Google.

3. Third-Party Services

Radioage uses the following third-party services to provide app functionality. These services may receive information such as your IP address as part of normal network requests:

  • Radio Browser API (api.radio-browser.info): A community-maintained directory used to retrieve radio station information and stream information.
  • GeoJS (geojs.io): An IP geolocation service used during the initial setup of Radioage to determine the user's approximate country.
  • RevenueCat: A subscription management service used to validate purchases, maintain subscription status, and manage access to Radioage PRO features.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies describing how they handle information received through their services.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Radioage does not sell or rent your personal information.

Radioage does not require an account and does not intentionally collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Limited information may nevertheless be transmitted to third-party service providers when necessary to provide app functionality. This includes:

  • Network information, including your IP address, when communicating with services such as Radio Browser and GeoJS.
  • Purchase and subscription information processed by RevenueCat to validate transactions and provide Radioage PRO features.
  • An anonymous RevenueCat App User ID used to associate purchase information with an anonymous customer record.

Radioage does not use this information for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

5. Data Retention

Radioage does not maintain a server-side database of your app preferences, favorites, themes, or language settings.

Information stored locally by Radioage remains on your device until you delete it by removing the app or clearing its local data.

Subscription and purchase information processed by RevenueCat is retained and managed by RevenueCat according to its applicable privacy policy and service terms.

6. Children's Privacy

Radioage does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App.
